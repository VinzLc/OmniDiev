Top-down 3/4 pixel art character sprite sheet, Game Boy Advance style (Pokemon Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald, 2002-2005).

CHARACTER: Lang, King of the Realm of Jade, a man of fifty who trains soldiers himself. Upright, square-shouldered, short iron-grey hair and a clipped grey beard. Weathered hands.

CLOTHES: a PALE JADE-GREEN robe of fine cloth over a darker green tunic, a wide dark sash, and a plain circlet of green stone. Understated: Jade has no army and does not dress like it does.

PALETTE — these colors only:
#0B0A10 #22222C #454652 (black, dark)
#0F3826 #1C7A4E #43C47F #8FE0AC (jade greens)
#3D2A12 #7A5223 (sash, leather)
#A6A8B2 #DDDDE4 (grey hair)
#A9714E #D9A57C #F2D3B0 (skin)

STYLE: flat fills only. Two-tone shading — one base color, one shadow, never a third. Hard dark outline around the whole figure. NO anti-aliasing, NO gradients, NO blur, NO soft shadow: every pixel is fully opaque or fully transparent. Chunky readable GBA proportions, large head, short limbs.

BACKGROUND: fully transparent. No scenery, no ground, no frame, no text, no watermark, no signature. Not 3D, not digital painting, not modern anime.
