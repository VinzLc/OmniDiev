Top-down 3/4 pixel art character sprite sheet, Game Boy Advance style (Pokemon Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald, 2002-2005).

CHARACTER: Santo, a Knight of Emerald and the Order's healer. A man of calm natural authority — the one others fall in behind without being told. Dark hair cut short, a clean-shaven face, steady eyes.

He carries a small HARP slung across his back on a leather strap, its frame of pale wood clearly visible over the black of his surcoat. He is the only Knight who carries one.

UNIFORM — identical for every Knight of Emerald: black tunic and black leather boots. Over them a two-tone surcoat split vertically, one half emerald green and one half black — a black cross on the green half, a green dragon on the black half. A gold cross set with emeralds on the breastplate. This uniform must read instantly at 32 px and must be the same on every Knight.

PALETTE — these 16 colors only:
#0B0A10 #22222C #454652 #71727E #DDDDE4 (black, stone)
#0F3826 #1C7A4E #43C47F (emerald green)
#3D2A12 #7A5223 #C08F34 #F0D174 (gold, leather)
#A9714E #D9A57C #F2D3B0 (skin)
#2D5FA8 (blue)

STYLE: flat fills only. Two-tone shading — one base color, one shadow, never a third. Hard dark outline around the whole figure. NO anti-aliasing, NO gradients, NO blur, NO soft shadow: every pixel is fully opaque or fully transparent. Chunky readable GBA proportions, large head, short limbs.

BACKGROUND: fully transparent. No scenery, no ground, no frame, no text, no watermark, no signature. Not 3D, not digital painting, not modern anime.
