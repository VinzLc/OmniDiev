Top-down 3/4 pixel art character sprite sheet, Game Boy Advance style (Pokemon Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald, 2002-2005).

CHARACTER: Élund, the old court magician of the Castle of Emerald, medieval fantasy. He reads omens in the sky and runs the school where gifted children are trained. Elderly, severe, impatient — a scholar who has spent forty years being right and resents having to explain.

Long grey beard reaching his chest. Thinning grey hair. Deep-set eyes under heavy brows, a permanent frown. Slightly stooped, one shoulder lower than the other.

CLOTHES: a long dark violet robe to the ankles, and over it a heavy grey travelling cape fastened at the throat with a plain silver clasp. No hat, no staff, no jewels.

PALETTE — these 14 colors only:

STYLE: flat fills only. Two-tone shading — one base color, one shadow, never a third. Hard dark outline around the whole figure. NO anti-aliasing, NO gradients, NO blur, NO soft shadow: every pixel is fully opaque or fully transparent. Chunky readable GBA proportions, large head, short limbs.

BACKGROUND: fully transparent. No scenery, no ground, no frame, no text, no watermark, no signature. Not 3D, not digital painting, not modern anime.
