Top-down 3/4 pixel art creature sprite sheet, Game Boy Advance style (Pokemon Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald, 2002-2005).

CREATURE: a war dragon of the Black Emperor, medieval dark fantasy. A heavy four-legged reptile, low and broad, built like a great lizard rather than a serpent — thick tail dragging behind, clawed feet, a long blunt snout with visible teeth. Folded leathery wings held tight against its flanks, not spread. Ridged spine running from skull to tail.

SKIN: dull violet-grey hide, darker along the back, paler on the belly. Cold pale eyes.

It must read at 32 pixels as a heavy predator: broad violet silhouette, low to the ground, clearly not human.

STYLE: flat fills only. Two-tone shading — one base color, one shadow, never a third. Hard dark outline around the whole creature. NO anti-aliasing, NO gradients, NO blur, NO soft shadow: every pixel is fully opaque or fully transparent.

BACKGROUND: fully transparent. No scenery, no ground, no rider, no frame, no text, no watermark, no signature. Not 3D, not digital painting, not modern anime.
