Top-down 3/4 pixel art character sprite sheet, Game Boy Advance style (Pokemon Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald, 2002-2005).

CHARACTER: Hamil, King of the Elves of Enkidiev, medieval fantasy. Tall and very slender, still, watchful. Long POINTED EARS rising through his hair. His EYES ARE GREEN — a clear leaf green, the most striking thing about him at this size.

Long straight pale silver-blond hair falling past his shoulders, unbound. A smooth ageless face, no beard.

CLOTHES: he is dressed in GREY from shoulder to boot, and in nothing else. The grey tunic of his people, soft and light, falls to mid-thigh over darker grey leggings; his boots are grey; his belt is grey leather. Every garment he wears is a shade of grey.

STYLE: flat fills only. Two-tone shading — one base color, one shadow, never a third. Hard dark outline around the whole figure. NO anti-aliasing, NO gradients, NO blur, NO soft shadow: every pixel is fully opaque or fully transparent. Chunky readable GBA proportions, large head, short limbs.

BACKGROUND: fully transparent. No scenery, no ground, no frame, no text, no watermark, no signature. Not 3D, not digital painting, not modern anime.
