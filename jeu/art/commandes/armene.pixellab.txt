Top-down 3/4 pixel art character sprite sheet, Game Boy Advance style (Pokemon Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald, 2002-2005).

CHARACTER: Armène, a servant of the Castle of Emerald who becomes its governess, medieval fantasy. A woman of about forty, solid and unhurried, with the steadiness of someone who has calmed a great many frightened children. A broad kind face, round cheeks, laugh lines. She is the only person in the castle who was not afraid of the mauve child.

Brown hair pulled back and covered by a plain linen headscarf, a few strands escaping.

CLOTHES: a simple long dress of undyed brown wool, and over it a pale linen apron tied at the waist, clean and often used. Flat leather shoes. Nothing rich — she is staff, not court.

PALETTE — these 14 colors only:

#3D2A12 #7A5223 #C08F34 (brown wool, leather)

STYLE: flat fills only. Two-tone shading — one base color, one shadow, never a third. Hard dark outline around the whole figure. NO anti-aliasing, NO gradients, NO blur, NO soft shadow: every pixel is fully opaque or fully transparent. Chunky readable GBA proportions, large head, short limbs.

BACKGROUND: fully transparent. No scenery, no ground, no frame, no text, no watermark, no signature. Not 3D, not digital painting, not modern anime.
