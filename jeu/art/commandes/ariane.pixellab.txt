Top-down 3/4 pixel art character sprite sheet, Game Boy Advance style (Pokemon Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald, 2002-2005).

CHARACTER: Ariane, a fae woman of twenty who gave up her wings for soldiering. NO WINGS AT ALL — her back is bare of them. Long pale silver-blond hair tied back in a single tail, very fine features, large light eyes, a narrow build that is wiry rather than frail. She stands lightly, weight forward.

CLOTHES: a black tunic and hose under a short sleeveless GREEN over-tunic, plain and undecorated. A single narrow leather belt. Soft low boots. No armour, no cross, no jewellery.

PALETTE — these colors only:
#0B0A10 #22222C #454652 #71727E (black tunic, hose)
#0F3826 #1C7A4E #43C47F (green over-tunic)
#A6A8B2 #DDDDE4 #F2F2F5 (silver-blond hair)
#3D2A12 #7A5223 (belt, boots)
#D9A57C #F2D3B0 (very pale skin)

STYLE: flat fills only. Two-tone shading — one base color, one shadow, never a third. Hard dark outline around the whole figure. NO anti-aliasing, NO gradients, NO blur, NO soft shadow: every pixel is fully opaque or fully transparent. Chunky readable GBA proportions, large head, short limbs.

BACKGROUND: fully transparent. No wings. No scenery, no ground, no frame, no text, no watermark, no signature. Not 3D, not digital painting, not modern anime.
