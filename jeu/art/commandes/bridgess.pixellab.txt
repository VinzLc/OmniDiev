Top-down 3/4 pixel art character sprite sheet, Game Boy Advance style (Pokemon Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald, 2002-2005).

CHARACTER: Bridgess, twelve years old, Squire to Wellan of the Knights of Emerald. A tall lanky girl, all elbows and long legs, standing very straight because she has been told to. BLONDE hair, straight and shoulder-length, tied back out of the way. A serious face that does not smile easily.
